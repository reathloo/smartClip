# SmartClip App-Icon

Erzeugt mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Tool. Original: `AppIcon.png` mit transparentem Außenbereich. macOS-Container: `AppIcon.icns`, Größen 16–1024 Pixel. Neu erstellen: `zsh scripts/build-icon.sh`.

## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS app icon for SmartClip, a private clipboard history app. Create one square 1024x1024 icon on a genuinely transparent canvas. Center a large macOS rounded-square tile occupying about 86% of the canvas with balanced margins. Muted deep petrol teal #237B70, restrained subtle tonal gradient, finely crafted softly raised surface, minimal modern native Mac aesthetic. Center one bold warm-white paperclip, diagonally tilted from lower left to upper right, clean rounded continuous strokes and generous negative space. Very subtle short shadow under the white clip for depth, strong clear silhouette readable at 16 pixels. No words, letters, badges, sparkles, extra objects, scene or mockup. Flat frontal view, no perspective. Outer canvas transparent, no white background, no checkerboard drawn. Sophisticated quiet utility app, matching a simple teal-and-neutral UI.
